## After each show

```
uv run lektyra claims <part of the title>      # the claims loop, replayed minute by minute (~$1.8 an hour)
uv run lektyra end-pass <part of the title>    # the whole-event pass: missed claims, links, duplicates
```

The ledger lands next to the audio (`claims-*.md`) and shows under the video's *Documents*.

## Costs

Words: ~$0.22 an hour (ElevenLabs). Voices: free (CPU). Claims: ~$1.8 an hour. Three shows ≈ $6.

## If something goes wrong

- The stream drops: ingest reconnects by itself (up to 20 times) and records the gap.
- The page shows an error: *Stop*, then *Start listening* again; it becomes a new video, nothing is lost.
- The server stops: `uv run lektyra ui` again; a session in progress is lost from that moment, the audio before is kept.
