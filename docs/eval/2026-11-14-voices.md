# Voices across events, 2026-11-14

**Question:** can a voice be recognised across videos years and rooms apart, so that voice profiles (ADR-004) are
worth building, and at what threshold?

**Data:**
- the five spike videos, 4 h in all, diarized whole by ADR-008: SpeechBrain ECAPA, our clustering, threshold 0.55,
  CPU, about 40 s per hour of audio;
- speakers with at least 2 % of their event;
- labels S1…N per event. **No names**: naming is Martin's (K16). The only fact used about the material is how it
  was chosen: one person appears in all five videos, two in both *V politike* duels (spike-plan §2).

## What it shows

1. **One voice is found in all five events** (A.S1 = B.S2 = C.S1 = D1.S1 = D2.S1), at 0.80–0.92. The scores hold:
   - across three years (2022, 2023, 2025);
   - across three rooms (studio, parliament hall, press room);
   - in the cross-talk of A.

   By how the material was chosen this is the person present in all five. **Martin confirms** (K16).
2. **The second voice of both duels** (A.S2 = B.S1, 0.92) matches nothing outside them (≤ 0.33).
3. **A's moderator is C's interviewer** (0.87). **B's moderator is someone else** (0.46).
4. **Same voice: 0.80–0.92. Different voices: 0.47 at most.** The gap of 0.33 leaves room for a safe threshold.

## Decision (provisional)

- Voice profiles are worth building with ECAPA as it is.
- **Threshold proposal**:
  - auto-name at ≥ 0.70;
  - suggest at 0.55–0.70;
  - unknown below 0.55.
  On this data that gives **zero false names** and finds the shared voices in every event.
- To revisit:
  - with s-norm (ADR-004) once there are more speakers for a cohort;
  - against pyannote/WeSpeaker embeddings when the token comes (ADR-008);
  - with more voices recorded in the same room. D2's journalists against D1's MPs top out at 0.47: same room
    type, different people.

**Limits:**
- 5 events and a handful of people. Real numbers need more voices.
- Overlap is not modelled (a window gets one speaker), so turn changes land up to a word or two off. The transcript
  snaps each change to the nearest pause.
- 2 % of speech with no clear speaker is labelled "?" rather than guessed.
